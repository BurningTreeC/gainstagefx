# Modern Micro B3 (Darkglass Microtubes B3K) research log

Engineering identity: **Darkglass Microtubes B3K** bass overdrive: a CMOS-inverter drive
circuit (descended from the EHX Hot Tubes) blended with a clean path, with Grunt and Attack
switches. Proposed stable id `pedal_darkglass_b3k`. Proposed display: **Modern Micro B3**.

Status: **RESEARCHED 2026-10-01, NOT CLEARED** (question 2). Not to be built from what is
available.

## Eight-question checkpoint

1. **Revision.** Several: the B3K v1, the v2, and board revisions (a "3.1" is mentioned);
   the B7K is a different pedal. Not decided, and not decidable without a trace.
2. **Original schematic found?** **No.** Darkglass publishes nothing, and every schematic
   in circulation was traced from a **clone kit** -- the widely shared one from a GUMA
   kit (Schalltechnik), drawn in EAGLE (peperspedals, 2017) -- not from a Darkglass unit.
   The original is surface-mount; builders say the circulating drawings involve
   "guessing" in component values and differ from the original board. The DIY projects
   (Aion FX Maelstrom and others) are adaptations of the same. The designer's own
   *predecessor*, the Microtubes 2K, was posted by Douglas Castro to DIYStompboxes in
   2008, but the B3K is not that circuit. Building from a clone is building from a near
   relative, which the rules forbid (`docs/models/studio_pre.md` stops in the same place).
3. **Best source.** None that qualifies.
4. **Cross-check.** Darkglass's manual gives the controls and switches only.
5-8. Not reached.

**To unblock:** a trace of a genuine B3K board, with photographs, by version.
