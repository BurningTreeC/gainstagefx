# Modern Generator and Modern 6L6 (Revv Generator 120) research log

Engineering identity: the **Revv Generator 120** (MK3 and earlier), a four-channel valve
head (Green, Blue, Red, Purple), five 12AX7s and a 12AU7 (the loop), four 6L6s with
Presence and Depth. Proposed stable ids `amp_revv_generator` and
`power_revv_generator_6l6`. Proposed display **Modern Generator** and **Modern 6L6**.

Status: **RESEARCHED 2026-10-02, NOT CLEARED** (question 2), the power stage with it.
Not to be built from what is available.

## Eight-question checkpoint

1. **Revision.** Several: the original Generator 120, the MK3 and the MK3 Rev. B. Not
   decidable without a drawing.
2. **Original schematic found?** **No.** Revv publishes owner's manuals only (ManualsLib
   carries the Generator 120's and the MK3's: controls, valve positions, bias test
   points, no circuit). Web searches of the amplifier forums (Music Electronics Forum,
   The Amp Garage, El34World) found no service schematic and no trace. Fractal's forum
   records that without one "Cliff has to manually trace the entire amp circuit by
   hand", and no trace by anyone has been published. **Not yet checked: elektrotanya**,
   whose search did not answer from here -- it is where the Vox V846 drawing turned up,
   and it should be searched with an account before this is final. (A search engine's
   summary said Revv gave its drawings to Line 6; the forum thread it cited does not
   say so, and it is not relied on.) The pedals Revv made of its
   channels (G3, G4) are op-amp circuits, not the valve amplifier, and building the
   head from them would be building from a near relative -- the rule forbids it.
3. **Best source.** None that qualifies.
4. **Cross-check.** The owner's manuals give controls, the Aggression switches, the valve
   complement and bias procedure: enough to check a drawing, not to replace one.
5-8. Not reached.

**What the chain does instead.** The modern chain's distortion is available as the
**Modern Purple** (the G3, cleared, `modern_purple.md`) -- Revv's own pedal of this head's
Purple channel -- into any of the existing power stages, and the **Modern 33** in front
of the existing high-gain amplifiers (the American 5150, the Cali Rectifier).

**To unblock:** Revv's service schematic for a stated revision, or a published trace of a
genuine unit with photographs.
